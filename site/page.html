KeySeal Source on GitHub

Sign documents with your USB token

KeySeal is a small, free app that lets a website use the signing certificate on your token or smart card. Your PIN and your key stay with you.

Download KeySeal

For Mac, Windows and Linux. Your computer will warn you the first time you open it; the guide below shows what to do.

All downloads

  • Mac Apple silicon and Intel, disk image Download
  • Windows 10 or 11 x64, zip with install.cmd Download
  • Linux, x64 AppImage, or the Debian and Ubuntu package AppImage .deb
  • Linux, arm64 AppImage, or the Debian and Ubuntu package AppImage .deb
